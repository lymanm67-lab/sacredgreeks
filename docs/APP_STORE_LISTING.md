## App Store Description (4000 chars max)

Sacred Greeks is the first-of-its-kind app designed for Christians navigating Greek-letter organizations. Whether you're in a Divine Nine (NPHC), NPC, IFC, or multicultural council, Sacred Greeks gives you biblical tools, daily devotionals, and a supportive community to help you honor your faith while embracing your Greek experience.

**DAILY DEVOTIONALS**
Start each day with scripture-based devotionals written specifically for Greeks. Explore how biblical principles apply to brotherhood, sisterhood, leadership, and service.

**PRAYER JOURNAL & PRAYER WALL**
Keep a private prayer journal or share requests on the community Prayer Wall. Track answered prayers and celebrate God's faithfulness together.

**DECISION GUIDE & ASSESSMENTS**
Use our PROOF Framework assessment to evaluate Greek life decisions through a biblical lens. Get personalized results with scripture references and actionable next steps.

**BIBLE STUDY TOOLS**
Search scripture by topic, explore Greek life symbols through a biblical lens, and dive into guided study content designed for chapter devotionals and personal growth.

**CHAPTER TOOLKIT**
Access resources for chapter chaplains, community service tracking, meeting notes, expense management, and leadership training — all faith-centered.

**COMMUNITY FEATURES**
- Member network to connect with faith-focused Greeks nationwide
- Discussion forums organized by topic and organization
- Events calendar for faith-based Greek gatherings
- Business directory supporting Greek-owned businesses

**PODCAST & MEDIA**
Listen to the Sacred Greeks podcast, explore video teachings, and access a library of articles on faith, identity, and Greek life.

**GAMIFICATION & STREAKS**
Earn points for daily engagement, unlock achievements, maintain devotional streaks, and celebrate milestones on your spiritual journey.

**ADDITIONAL FEATURES**
• Myth Buster: Biblical perspectives on common Greek life misconceptions
• Symbol Guide: Explore the spiritual significance of Greek symbols
• Anti-Hazing Resources: Faith-based approach to standing against hazing
• Financial Stewardship: Sacred Money Course for chapter treasurers
• Healing Resources: Support for those processing difficult Greek life experiences

Sacred Greeks is built by Dr. Alton Lyman, a Greek life educator and ordained minister who has spent decades helping Christians thrive in Greek organizations.

Download Sacred Greeks today and discover how your faith and Greek life can work together for God's glory.
